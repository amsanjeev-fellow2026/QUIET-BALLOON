🔊 What voice level do we need?
Measures overall room loudness, not who is talking or what is said.

📱 Use your phone as the mic

📺 On the SmartTV / Smartboard / laptop

1Open this website and tap Play on any game (for example Quiet Balloon).
2Look at the top right corner. You will see a QR code and a 4-digit Phone code. (If it is hidden, tap 📱 Phone code ▸ to open it.)

📱 On your phone

3Open the same website in the phone's browser. Or just scan the QR code with the phone camera and skip to step 5.
4On the home page tap "📱 I'm on a phone".
5Type the 4-digit code from the big screen and tap Connect.
6Tap Allow when the phone asks for the microphone.

✅ Done!

7The big screen says "Phone mic connected!". Put the phone near the class and keep its screen on.
🔒 The phone sends only a loudness number, never sound. Both devices need internet.

How does this work?
  1. On the SmartTV or Smartboard, open the website and tap Play on a game.
  2. Find the 4-digit Phone code at the top right of that screen.
  3. Type it here and tap Connect, then allow the microphone.
  4. Keep this phone near the class with its screen on.
